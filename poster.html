✓ ON Good → Step 2

2

✓ LOCK OFF Good → Step 3

3

✗ 1-2 LEDs Tank reheating — wait 10–20 min for all 3 LEDs to fill up
✓ 3 LEDs Ready! Test with LEFT lever within 7 sec of pressing Hot
